Build notes (not part of the screen)

Why
An incident's full story (who ticked which step and when, IT's notes, the block decision, the backup result) is in AlertOut, but Right now shows it in separate pieces and drops the incident once it closes. Staff and IT have no single page to point at either.
Opens from
History rows, the headline of each card on Right now, “Closed today” rows (OwnerNow.jsx:430), and the IT panel's ticket title.
Data
No single-alert route exists, so find the alert by id in GET /alerts?role=owner&include_closed=true. Use GET /actions filtered by alert_id for block rows, and GET /restore/latest with derive.restoreSince for the backup line. The timeline is activity() filtered to this alert, plus the restore run.
Reuse
Checklist, ItCard and CertIn from OwnerNow.jsx; export them instead of copying. RULE_DESC from content.js for the technical details.
States
Id gone after a Reset: “This incident was cleared by a demo reset” with a link to History. Closed: a green “Closed” pill and IT's fix note at the top; the timeline ends with “You confirmed the fix”.
Safety
IT notes are free text typed by a person: render them as text nodes only, like log_fields.
