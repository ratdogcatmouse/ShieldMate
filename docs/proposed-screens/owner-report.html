Build notes (not part of the screen)

Why
The CERT-In card already promises it: “Next step after the demo: a report drafted from this timeline, for you to check and send” (OwnerNow.jsx:295). Two card steps, REPORT_CERTIN and START_DPDP_NOTE, have no screen behind them.
When it shows
For lessons with a CERT-In clock (CERTIN_HOURS: ransomware) and cards with a REPORT_CERTIN step (files_copied_out). The breach-record tab shows for cards with START_DPDP_NOTE, or when “Was patient data involved?” is Yes.
Data
Same as Incident detail, nothing new: the alert, its steps and IT log, GET /restore/latest, and clinic details from GET /clinic.
Output
“Copy as text” via navigator.clipboard.writeText; “Print” via window.print() with a print stylesheet that hides the nav. Nothing is sent from ShieldMate.
“I've sent it”
Ticks REPORT_CERTIN through the existing PUT /alerts/{id}/steps/REPORT_CERTIN where the card has that step. The ransomware card has no such step, so remembering “sent” there needs storage (a contract change). The button can be left out until then.
Privacy
What the owner types stays in the browser. It is never posted to the event log (CLAUDE.md: event payloads never hold free text).
Open
Check the field list against CERT-In's current incident form before building. Confirm with an advisor what the patient-data breach record must hold and by when; the mockup shows fields only.
