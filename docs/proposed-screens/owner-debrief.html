Build notes (not part of the screen)

Why
After a practice attack, Right now and staff Today show one line (SimResult). GET /status → simulation carries much more: start and finish times, stages fired and cancelled, the stop actions, and who contained it and when. Nothing shows the team how it went.
Data
  • GET /status → simulation (SimSummary), with derive.simResult() for the headline.
  • The cards it raised: alerts with first_seen between started_at and finished_at.
  • Who saw what: each role's list, using derive.stepProgress() as Mission control does.
  • Habit wording: ACTION_LABEL for contained_by.action and stop_actions.
Opens from
The SimResult card on Right now and staff Today (make it a link), and Backstage's “Last run” line.
Needs a field (optional)
