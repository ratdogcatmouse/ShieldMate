How it behaves

  • The “?” is on screen only while at least one alert or action card is showing. “No cards” hides it. It is never on Mission control.
  • Pressing it opens a bottom sheet listing every card currently on screen. Nothing is typed.
  • Tick one or more, press Rephrase. The sheet closes and a “In simpler words” block appears under each ticked card, with a Hide button.
  • The original wording on the card is never replaced or removed.
  • Pressing “?” again lets them pick other cards, or the same one again.
  • Failures stay on the card they belong to and say the original wording is still valid.

Choices I made that you didn't specify

  • Multi-select (checkboxes), because you wrote “alerts/cards”. Single-select is a one-line change.
  • Inline result under the card rather than inside the sheet, so the person reads it next to the original.
  • Approval cards (e.g. “Block an attacker?”) are rephrasable too. Their Approve / Don't buttons stay untouched.
  • Works for both staff and owner phones; same “?” and sheet.
